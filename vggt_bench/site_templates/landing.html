Sparse-view 3D reconstruction: VGGT vs. COLMAP

How many photos does it take to recover camera poses? This project compares VGGT, a feed-forward transformer, with classical COLMAP on $min_views to $max_views views of $n_scenes Mip-NeRF 360 scenes, then runs both on phone videos. Everything ran on one RTX 5070 Laptop GPU; COLMAP ran on the CPU.

Results

$results

Reference poses are the dataset's own COLMAP reconstruction (pseudo-ground-truth), which may favour COLMAP. Runtimes compare a GPU model with CPU-only COLMAP, so they are not like for like.
