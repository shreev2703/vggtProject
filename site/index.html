Sparse-view 3D reconstruction: VGGT vs. COLMAP

How many photos does it take to recover camera poses? This project compares VGGT, a feed-forward transformer, with classical COLMAP on 3 to 40 views of 6 Mip-NeRF 360 scenes, then runs both on phone videos. Everything ran on one RTX 5070 Laptop GPU; COLMAP ran on the CPU.
